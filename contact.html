Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days. Express delivery is available at checkout for 1–2 business day shipping.

What is your return policy?

We offer hassle-free returns within 15 days of delivery. Products must be unused and in original packaging.

Do you ship across India?

Yes! We ship to 500+ cities and towns across India. Enter your pin code at checkout to confirm availability.

How do I track my order?

Once shipped, you'll receive an SMS and email with your tracking link. You can also track from your account dashboard.
